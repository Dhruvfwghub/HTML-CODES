WELCOME TO THE COLLEGE DHRUV PRATAP SINGH RAJPUT JI Dear Parents and Well-wishers,

For the past 54 years, we have upheld a proud tradition as one of Madhya Pradesh’s leading higher education institutions—empowering thousands of students each year. This year, too, has been no exception, as we continue to give wings to the aspirations and futures of our learners. We have had normal admissions and the year took off quite smoothly now that we have established ourselves as pioneers in implementing NEP 2020. The odd semester exams too are over and the performance of the students has been beyond expectations proving the dedication of our teachers and other staff which is beyond words. Congratulations to our students, staff, and parents on the successful completion of the semester!

Our Vision
To lead as an institution of excellence in learning and empower future leaders by transforming knowledge into affirmative action.

Our Mission

The college intends: Build a strong value base among students, inculcate the spirit of patriotism and bridge the rural-urban divide. Provide a vibrant, inspiring, professional and academic ambience. Respond to the needs of an evolving society through appropriate programs and innovative pedagogy. Build capacities for employability, entrepreneurship and research with focus on creativity and innovation. Create and disseminate knowledge through Research, Consultancy, Publications, Networking and Campus/outreach programs.

Our Values
  • Inclusiveness, Synergy, Quality, Ethics and Integrity.
  • Faith in and Inspiration from the cumulative wisdom of our traditions and heritage.
  • Stewardship of nature and environment for a sustainable development of the world for the generations to come.
  • Respect for and integral development of every human person.
  • Universal brotherhood of humanity inspired by the prayer Our Father in Heaven prompted by the legacy of Jesus Christ.
  • Freedom coupled with responsibility and discipline.
  • Learning and teaching to be a lifelong mission.
